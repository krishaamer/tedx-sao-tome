# Google Drive edition evidence

Research date: 2026-10-14. This was a read-only discovery and extraction pass.
No cloud document was modified, shared, moved or made public. The website uses
historical publication material, not private correspondence or draft statistics.

## Coverage

- Fully paginated `TEDx` document search: 230 returned metadata records over
  three provider pages; no next-page token remained.
- Fully paginated `TEDxSãoTomé` document search: 155 returned metadata records
  over two provider pages; no next-page token remained.
- The union contains 306 unique file IDs. Many are duplicate exports, other
  TEDx events, personal CVs or unrelated documents mentioning TEDx; this is
  not a count of 306 independent São Tomé sources.
- `Futurismo` discovery yielded two unrelated later notes, not new programme
  evidence. A folder search found 19 matching folders and no remaining token.
- Direct metadata discovery in the recovered September 2018 speaker-profile
  folder found three profiles documents. The Mé-Chinhô source folder contained
  the base script, a September 22 slide deck and a speaker guide. Both folder
  searches completed without a next-page token.

These searches cover accessible indexed matches, not every file in the
account. Source relevance was assessed before fetching content. Unrelated
resumes, identity/contact records, private conversation exports and broad
numeric attachment collections were not bulk-published. The local and Dropbox
passes supply programmes and biographies that keyword discovery alone missed.

## Decisions

The final WordPress programme and contemporary post-event records remain the
stronger evidence for September 29, 2018, CACAU and its 13 participants.
Preparation lists are preserved as source discrepancies, not merged into one
purported final roster. Historical biographies are explicitly dated on the
website. Private source links in this research report do not imply public
access and are not exposed as playable media URLs.
