## 2013: contemporary organizer account and stage photographs

The local Medium export is
`/Users/krishaamer/Work/_me/web-data/medium/medium-2026-02-19/posts/2013-09-20_TEDxS-oTom---The-Story-Of-The-Event-Of-The-Year-In-S-o-Tom--and-Pr-ncipe-1bc9bcf226a3.html`.
Line 67 contains the organizer's retrospective, speaker sections, photographs
and team roles. Its canonical public article is
[TEDxSãoTomé: The Story Of The Event Of The Year](https://medium.com/@krishaamer/tedxsaotome-the-story-of-the-event-of-the-year-in-sao-tome-and-principe-1bc9bcf226a3).
It explicitly dates the event June 20, 2013 and names the Islands Connected
theme. It is an organizer account, not independent attendance measurement.

Contemporary June 20 Instagram captions independently identify Nelly Strong,
Dynka Amorim, Ismael Sequeira with Dio Lima, Robert Drewes, Mark Shuttleworth,
Aoani d'Alva, Maria do Céu Madureira and Sara Moreira on stage. These match the
existing official recording archive. The `The-We-Anthology/lang/en.i18n.json`
lines 56–58 also preserve June 20, 2013 and the Islands Connected description.

The organizer article supplies useful historical biography summaries (roles
are specifically as described in 2013, not verified current appointments):

- Ismael Sequeira: painter and co-founder of Plataforma Cafuka, working across
  arts, museology and multimedia; his contribution concerns artistic
  innovation and connections across Lusophone creative communities.
- Dynka Amorim: youth activist and founder of Bué Fixe, whose youth-oriented
  work addresses HIV prevention and diaspora issues; the talk reflects on
  community action and social entrepreneurship.
- Mark Shuttleworth: software entrepreneur associated with Canonical and
  HBD; his contribution connects nature protection and ecotourism with the
  islands' future. Avoid repeating unverified investment totals.
- Nelly Strong: musician/rapper Marlene Bandeira, whose performance and
  personal narrative connect music, expression and resilience.
- Sara Moreira: then Portuguese-language editor at Global Voices with a
  background in computing and citizen media; her contribution concerns
  communication, citizen journalism and listening across communities.
- Guilherme de Carvalho: musician, composer and founding member of Grupo
  Tempo; the article identifies a musical performance.
- Maria do Céu Madureira: pharmacist/researcher whose ethnopharmacological
  work documents medicinal plants and traditional knowledge in the islands.
- Robert Drewes: biologist/herpetologist with African field-research
  experience; his contribution concerns island biodiversity.
- Aoaní d'Alva: São Tomé-born journalist who trained in Brazil and worked
  in Angola; her contribution compares the islands with mainland Africa.
