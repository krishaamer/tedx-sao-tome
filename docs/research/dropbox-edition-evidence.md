# Dropbox edition evidence

Research date: 14 October 2026. Read-only inspection of the connected Dropbox account. No files were moved, changed, shared, or downloaded, and no new sharing links were created.

This report records editorial evidence and discrepancies, not a public export of the organisation's working files. Cloud source links were returned by Dropbox's existing extraction service; access may require the account's normal permissions. The website dataset names archived sources but does not expose these cloud links.

## Editorial rules

- Use a printed/published programme or a dated later website document ahead of an earlier planning list.
- Describe programme times as announced times, not measured start times.
- Do not imply that an invitation, speaker folder, or announcement proves a completed presentation.
- Keep the original title announced in the programme separate from the eventual recording title.
- Label TED video screenings separately from talks delivered at TEDxSãoTomé.
- Historical roles and biographies describe the relevant edition, not current appointments.
- Do not publish contact/attendee lists, application or release forms, passport scans, invoices, budgets, internal assessments, or correspondence as archive-page content.
